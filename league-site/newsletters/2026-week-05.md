Five weeks into the season, and the fantasy gods have once again reminded us that nobody is safe.

For the first time all season, Brendan has tasted defeat. The league's last undefeated team has officially fallen, and Pisskissing Supreme has taken his place atop the standings.

Meanwhile, Darian is back and apparently has no intention of slowing down, Manning finally put everything together, and Joe's Scary Team delivered its best performance of the season.

Even Deez Fadez, the league's most notorious fraud, finally managed to score more than 100 points.

A historic achievement for a man who spent the first four weeks winning games with the offensive production of a middle school JV team.

And then there's Mike.

Five weeks. Five losses.

It has been wild thus far, so let's get into it.

## 👑 Down Goes the Leader, Brendan's Luck Finally Runs Out: Pisskissing Supreme 123.16 — Team Forde 90.26

It finally happened.

After four weeks of sitting comfortably atop the standings, Brendan's reign of terror has come to an end.

For the first month of the season, everything seemed to go Brendan's way. Close games, favorable matchups, and just enough points to keep his perfect record alive.

But as the saying goes, it's better to be lucky than good.

Unfortunately for Brendan, his luck finally ran out.

Coming into Week 5, he was the league's only undefeated team. He had survived every challenge thrown his way.

Then he scored 90.26 points.

And waiting on the other side was Pisskissing Supreme.

Isaiah Likely exploded for 32.4 points, Parker Washington added 30.4, and Kyler Murray contributed another 18.26 as Pisskissing Supreme cruised to a 123.16 point victory.

Meanwhile, Brendan's offense completely collapsed.

Trey McBride led the team with 21.6 points, Brock Purdy added 16.16, and Bijan Robinson managed just 15.8.

Both teams now sit at 4-1, but Pisskissing Supreme takes over first place.

The undefeated dream is dead.

Brendan's luck has run out.

## 🔥 Darian Is Back and He's Not Taking His Foot Off the Gas: Johnson 165.06 — Team Payne 123.36

Ladies and gentlemen, Darian is officially back.

Last week, Johnson put up 148.66 points and announced his return to relevance.

Apparently, that was just the warm up.

This week, Darian dropped a ridiculous **165.06 points** on Team Payne, marking his second consecutive performance above 148.

Drake Maye erupted for 39.46 points, Jaylen Warren added 36.8, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba contributed another 22.8.

Payne didn't exactly have a terrible week, either.

Jayden Daniels finished with 32.26 points, while Schrader and Derrick Henry each added 21.

But 123.36 points wasn't even remotely enough.

Darian wins by 41.70 points, improving to 3-2 and delivering one of the most convincing performances of the season.

Two weeks ago, Darian was sitting at 1-2, and nobody was talking about him as a serious contender.

Now he's won two straight games while averaging nearly 157 points.

And judging by the way things are going, he has absolutely no intention of slowing down.

Darian is back, and the rest of the league has officially been put on notice.

## 📦 Manning Didn't Forget About Big Loadz: Team Manning 165.40 — Team Szub 116.60

After four weeks of disappointment, injuries, and questionable decision making, Manning finally had a week where he tied everything together.

The players showed up. The lineup decisions paid off. And for once, everything actually went according to plan.

Unfortunately for Max, he was on the receiving end.

Jahmyr Gibbs exploded for 37.7 points, Bucky Irving added another 31.5, and Joe Burrow contributed 24.6.

The result?

**165.40 points.**

The highest score in the entire league this week, narrowly edging Darian by just 0.34 points.

Max actually put together a respectable 116.6 point performance behind Josh Allen's 30.5, Matthew Golden's 20.3, and Ja'Marr Chase's 19.7.

But Manning wasn't interested in a close game.

And despite Max's attempt to rebrand as Team Szub, the commissioner would like to remind everyone that this franchise was once known as Big Loadz.

Last week, Max couldn't produce a big enough load to beat a team that scored 89 points.

This week, Manning gave him a load so big he probably won't be able to walk straight until Week 6.

Manning wins by 48.80 points, the largest margin of victory in Week 5, and improves to 2-3.

For the first time this season, Manning finally looked like a team capable of competing with anyone in the league.

As for Max, changing your team name doesn't erase your history.

Once Big Loadz, always Big Loadz.

## 🚨 Breaking News, Deez Fadez Discovers the Triple Digits: Deez Fadez 137.00 — 5280 110.94

Alert the authorities.

Deez Fadez has scored more than 100 points in a fantasy football game.

Yes, you read that correctly.

After spending four consecutive weeks somehow winning games while barely fielding a functioning offense, the league's most notorious fraud has finally put together a legitimate performance.

George Pickens led the way with 28 points, Matthew Stafford added 27.32, and Tyler Warren contributed another 21.4.

The result was a shocking 137 point performance.

5280, coming off a 152 point explosion last week, could only manage 110.94 points this time around.

Jared Goff led the team with 28.94 points, but Christian McCaffrey's 17.3 wasn't nearly enough to keep pace.

Deez Fadez improves to 4-1.

That's right. Four wins in five weeks.

Last week, we were demanding a federal investigation into how this man kept winning games with fewer than 100 points.

Now he's actually scoring points.

This is a deeply troubling development.

If Deez Fadez has figured out how to combine his supernatural ability to win close games with an actual functioning offense, the rest of the league may be in serious trouble.

As for Carson, he falls to 2-3 but is still in control of his own destiny.

## 😈 The Defending Champion's Nightmare Continues: Joe's Scary Team 159.44 — Mele 124.84

Someone might want to check on the defending champion.

Because whatever championship magic Mele possessed last season appears to have completely vanished.

Despite putting together a respectable 124.84 point performance, Mele was crushed by Joe's Scary Team.

Mike Evans led Joe with 26.3 points, James Cook III added 22.8, and the San Francisco defense delivered another 20.

Joe finished with 159.44 points, the third highest total in a week filled with offensive explosions.

Mele, meanwhile, got 37.7 points from Puka Nacua, 23 from Brock Bowers, and 21.74 from Trevor Lawrence.

On most weeks, that's a pretty solid lineup.

Unfortunately, Joe finally decided to live up to his team's name.

The defending champion falls to 1-4, while Joe improves to 2-3.

We're now five weeks into the season, and Mele has won exactly one game.

We might have to stop referring to him as the defending champion and start referring to him as the former champion here pretty soon.

## 🐙 The Kraken Consumes a Corpse of a Team: Kraken 109.98 — Madison Beers Good Boy 103.64

Ladies and gentlemen, Mike is 0-5.

Five weeks into the season, and this man has still not experienced the sensation of winning a fantasy football game.

And this week, he came agonizingly close.

The Kraken entered Week 5 having lost two straight games and desperately needed a victory.

Christian Watson delivered 32.3 points, Jacoby Brissett contributed 25.28, and Davante Adams added another 22.1.

The Kraken finished with 109.98 points.

Mike, meanwhile, received 29.1 points from Malik Nabers and 20.94 from C.J. Stroud.

He finished with 103.64.

A difference of just **6.34 points**.

That's it.

Six points stood between Mike and his first victory of the season.

Instead, the Kraken escapes with a win, improves to 3-2, and avoids the embarrassment of becoming the first team to lose to Mike.

Let's also not ignore the fact that the Kraken needed everything he had to beat a team that hasn't won a single game.

The Kraken may have survived, but this wasn't exactly an inspiring performance.

As for Mike, the historic pursuit of 0-14 continues.

We are witnessing history, gentlemen.

And not the good kind.
