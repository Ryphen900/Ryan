After last week's absolute bloodbath, Week 3 was at least a little kinder to us.

A little.

There were fewer injuries, fewer lineups getting nuked before halftime, and fewer owners watching their seasons die in real time.

But it was not without sacrifice.

Since the last newsletter, we learned that Jaxson Dart's season is over with a torn MCL, and this week De'Von Achane went down with a torn ACL, adding two more names to the growing list of brothers who have since joined Valhalla.

To my friends Jaxson Dart and De'Von Achane: rest now, brothers. We have the watch, and I'll see you in Valhalla.

As for the rest of the league, Week 3 finally gave us some separation.

Brendan is now the last undefeated man standing at 3-0. Mike is the only winless team left at 0-3.

The defending champ finally woke up. Manning avoided joining Mike in the basement. Deez Fadez somehow has a winning record without scoring 100 points once.

And then there was Johnson versus Big Loadz.

We'll get to that.

## 💦 Johnson Shoots His Load All Over Big Loadz: Johnson 143.60 — Big Loadz 102.86

There really wasn't any other way to title this one.

Johnson came into Week 3, saw a team named Big Loadz, and gave Max exactly what his team name promised.

Darian spent the entire afternoon shooting loads all over Max's lineup until the whole damn team was covered. Johnson finished with a league best **143.6 points**, led by JSN dropping 35.36.

Fittingly, this was also the Blowout of the Week.

Darian blew Max out by 40.74 points, and it was one hell of a pounding from start to finish. All Max could do was sit there and take it like a good boy.

By Sunday night, Max wasn't even fighting back anymore. He was just lying there staring at the ceiling fan, wondering how a team called Big Loadz somehow ended up being the one covered in them.

Max actually got respectable performances from Ja'Marr Chase and Jordan Love, but it didn't matter. Once Johnson started spraying points everywhere, there was no stopping the mess.

Big Loadz came in looking to make one. Instead, Johnson finished all over the scoreboard.

## 🍀 Forde Continues to Get Away With It: Team Forde 127.14 — 5280 126.46

At this point, somebody needs to investigate Brendan.

Last week, Forde scored 85 points and somehow walked away with a win.

This week, Carson puts up 126.46, and Brendan survives by exactly **0.68 points**.

That's it.

Less than one point.

Carson could have gotten a win with this performance any other week, and instead he's sitting here with another loss.

And somehow, after all of that, Brendan is now 3-0 and the only undefeated team left in the entire league.

One week he wins with 85.

The next week he wins by less than a point.

Meanwhile, everybody else has already taken at least one punch to the mouth.

At this point, I'm not even sure Brendan has to be good. He just has to keep showing up and letting increasingly ridiculous shit happen around him.

He could score 74 next week and somehow win.

Twelve teams entered. One undefeated bastard remains.

## 🏆 The Champ Finally Wakes Up: Mele 126.58 — Kraken 112.08

It took three weeks, but the defending champion is finally alive.

After starting the season 0-2, Mele finally got himself into the win column, and he did it by knocking off one of the hotter teams in the league.

The Kraken has been slain.

Mele put together his best performance of the season and finally looked a little more like the guy who won the whole damn thing last year.

Brock Bowers led the way with 27.6, and this time Mele finally got enough from the rest of the lineup to finish the job.

Kraken wasn't terrible either. He still put up 112.08.

It just wasn't enough.

Maybe the champ was just hungover for the first two weeks.

The title defense officially has a pulse.

## 😮‍💨 Manning Avoids the Dreaded 0-3 Start: Team Manning 136.48 — Joe's Scary Team 113.44

Team Manning desperately needed this one.

There's 0-2, where you can still tell yourself everything is fine.

Then there's 0-3, where you start really worrying.

Manning avoided all of that with a huge 136.48 point performance and finally got himself into the win column.

Jahmyr Gibbs went absolutely nuclear for **41.4 points**, and Manning finally got the kind of week he probably thought this roster was capable of when he drafted it.

There is, however, one gigantic fucking problem.

Through three weeks, Manning's tight ends have combined for...

1.3 points.

Through three full weeks.

At this point, Manning isn't even really starting a tight end. He's basically playing fantasy football with one less position than everybody else and hoping the rest of the lineup can carry the corpse.

This week, they did.

If Manning ever finds a tight end capable of catching a football and remaining conscious afterward, this team might actually be dangerous.

Until then, he's basically playing on hard mode.

## 🚽 How Does He Keep Getting Away With This? Deez Fadez 89.90 — Madison Beers Good Boy 86.74

I genuinely do not understand how this keeps happening.

Deez Fadez is 2-1.

That sounds respectable.

Then you actually look at the scores.

Through three weeks, this team has yet to score 100 points in a single game.

Not once.

And somehow he has a winning record.

This week, Deez Fadez managed just 89.9 points and still won because Michael somehow managed to shit the bed even harder.

This didn't really feel like a fantasy football matchup.

It was just two homeless guys fighting over the last cigarette behind a gas station dumpster.

Somebody technically had to win.

And while Deez somehow crawls out of another dumpster fire at 2-1, Michael falls to 0-3, making him the only winless team left in the league.

Now the season starts getting uncomfortable.

At 0-2, you can still tell yourself it's early.

At 0-3, you start staring at your bench, thinking about trades you would have laughed at two weeks ago, and wondering how much longer you can wait before smashing the panic button.

Michael isn't dead yet.

But he had better start salvaging what's left of this season quickly.
