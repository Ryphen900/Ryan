Four weeks down, and this league continues to defy both logic and the laws of probability.

Brendan remains undefeated at 4-0, Mike remains winless at 0-4, and Deez Fadez continues to commit crimes against fantasy football.

We now have a team sitting at 3-1 that has yet to score 100 points in a single game. Four weeks. Four sub 100 performances. Three victories.

Meanwhile, other teams are putting up 120 plus points and losing.

This week also saw Team Payne continue his impressive start, Johnson fight his way back to .500, and the Kraken suffer yet another humiliating defeat.

And somehow, despite everything, the defending champion has managed to fall right back to 1-3.

Let's get into it.

## 🚔 The Greatest Fraud in Fantasy Football History: Deez Fadez 89.38 — Big Loadz 87.82

Ladies and gentlemen, we may be witnessing something that will never be replicated in the history of this game.

Deez Fadez is 3-1.

He has scored under 100 points every single week, and he just won another game with 89.38 points. It is also worth mentioning that he has scored the fewest total points in the league by a mile.

To put this into perspective, nine of the twelve teams in this league outscored him this week, and yet he walks away with another victory.

Max deserves some blame here, too. All he had to do was score 90 points. Ninety. Instead, he put up 87.82 and somehow managed to lose to a team that should be under federal investigation.

For a man named Big Loadz, Max showed up with an embarrassingly small load this week. And when it mattered most, he just couldn't make it big enough to get the job done.

The final margin was **1.56 points**.

Deez Fadez continues to launder wins.

## 🐠 The Kraken Has Officially Been Placed on the Endangered Species List: Team Forde 120.18 — Kraken 89.68

Remember when the Kraken was one of the most feared teams in the league?

Those were simpler times.

After getting knocked off by the defending champion last week, the Kraken has now suffered another humiliating defeat, this time at the hands of the league's only remaining undefeated team.

Brendan casually put up 120.18 points and watched the Kraken drown himself with 89.68.

Bijan Robinson led Forde with 27.7 points, while the Kraken was carried by a 25.9 point performance from Chuba Hubbard.

Brendan is now 4-0 and sitting comfortably atop the standings.

The Kraken, once a terrifying force of nature, is now 2-2 and looking more like a slightly aggressive goldfish.

## 📢 Johnson Puts the League on Notice With a 148 Point Statement Win: Johnson 148.66 — Team Manning 124.42

After finally picking up his first victory in Week 3, Manning entered Week 4 hoping to build some momentum.

Instead, Johnson decided to put on an absolute clinic.

Kyren Williams exploded for 36.7 points, Zay Flowers added 25.8, and Bryce Young somehow contributed another 21.46.

Johnson finished with 148.66 points, the second highest total of the week.

Manning, meanwhile, put together a respectable 124.42 point performance behind Nico Collins and Joe Burrow.

In most matchups, that's enough to win.

Not this one.

Johnson improves to 2-2, fighting his way back to .500 and reminding the rest of the league that he's still very much in the championship conversation.

Manning falls to 1-3, and his Week 3 comeback already feels like a distant memory.

## 💀 Mike's Historic Pursuit of 0-14 Remains Alive: 5280 152.08 — Madison Beers Good Boy 123.18

Four weeks.

Four losses.

Zero signs of life.

Mike entered Week 4 searching for his first victory and actually managed to put up 123.18 points.

For a brief moment, we thought this might finally be the week. It was a performance that would have beaten several teams in this league.

Unfortunately, 5280 decided this was the perfect week to have the highest scoring performance of Week 4.

Jameson Williams exploded for 31.3 points, Tee Higgins contributed 26.7, and Jared Goff added another 20.48.

The result was a 152.08 point demolition.

Mike is now 0-4, dead last in the standings, and running out of excuses.

At this point, the question is no longer whether Mike can make the playoffs.

It's whether he'll show any signs of a pulse before Halloween.

## 👻 Joe's Scary Team Finally Gets Scary. Unfortunately, So Does His Opponent: Team Payne 146.30 — Joe's Scary Team 134.00

Joe's Scary Team finally showed some signs of life this week.

Tetairoa McMillan erupted for **45.2 points**, Sam LaPorta added 22.4, and Dak Prescott contributed another 18.1.

Joe finished with 134 points.

Surely, that would be enough.

Unfortunately, Team Payne had CeeDee Lamb.

Lamb went nuclear for 41.3 points, Wilson added another 27, and Patrick Mahomes chipped in 17.

Payne finished with 146.3 points to improve to 3-1 and move into second place.

Joe falls to 1-3 despite putting together one of the better performances of Week 4.

For the first time all season, Joe's Scary Team actually lived up to its name.

The only problem is that nobody remembered to tell Team Payne he was supposed to be scared.

## 🏚️ The Defending Champion's Comeback Lasted Exactly One Week: Pisskissing Supreme 119.48 — Mele 102.58

Last week, Mele finally got his first win of the season, knocking off the Kraken and giving the league reason to believe the defending champion was back.

That lasted approximately seven days.

Pisskissing Supreme handled business behind Kenneth Walker's 30.9 points and Jonathan Taylor's 22.7, cruising to a 119.48 to 102.58 victory.

Mele received solid contributions from Puka Nacua and Brock Bowers, but it wasn't nearly enough.

Pisskissing Supreme improves to 3-1 and remains firmly in the championship conversation.

Mele falls to 1-3.

The defending champion's title defense is beginning to resemble a government funded rebuilding project. Plenty of promises, very little to show for it.

## 🏅 The Commissioner's Week 4 Awards

**Team of the Week: 5280.** A league leading 152.08 points and a nearly 29 point victory over Mike. A thoroughly dominant performance.

**Blowout of the Week: Team Forde.** A 30.50 point victory over the Kraken. The supposed sea monster never stood a chance.

This league has already proven that common sense has no place here.
