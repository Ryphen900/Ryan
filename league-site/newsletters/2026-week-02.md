Two weeks into the season, and I sure as hell hope we don't get another week like that one.

If your team walked away from Week 2 without somebody getting hurt, consider yourself lucky. It felt like half the league got taken out. Jayden Daniels, Caleb Williams, Saquon Barkley, Jaxson Dart, Alec Pierce, and Jadarian Price were just a few of the casualties, with several of them looking like they'll miss some time.

And somehow, the injuries weren't even the ugliest part.

This was one of the most pathetic offensive weeks we've seen in a while, and the fantasy scores reflected it. Five teams failed to crack 100 points. We had one matchup where neither team deserved to win and two others decided by less than a single point.

After all the carnage, four teams remain undefeated with the dream of a perfect season technically still alive, while two teams sit at 0-2 desperately searching for their first win.

With all that being said, let's dive in.

## 👑 Still on Top: Pisskissing Supreme 134.46 — Team Manning 93.68

Pisskissing Supreme continues his dominance.

After his receivers failed to perform in Week 1, the roster looked much more complete this time around. The matchup stayed relatively close for most of Sunday before Kenneth Walker and Jonathan Taylor decided to slam the door shut.

Taylor finished with 29.2, Walker added 23.8, and Jaylen Waddle finally joined the party with 21.8.

More importantly, Colin somehow survived the Week 2 injury apocalypse without losing half his roster.

Team Manning wasn't so fortunate on the scoreboard.

Jahmyr Gibbs and Garrett Wilson both turned in respectable performances, but there wasn't nearly enough behind them. Carnell Tate disappointed, and Colston Loveland has now managed a breathtaking **1.3 total points** through two weeks at tight end.

At this point, saying Loveland is playing the TE position is generous.

With Nico Collins out, Manning desperately needed somebody else to step up.

Nobody did.

Pisskissing Supreme moves to 2-0 and currently looks like the most complete team in the league.

Team Manning falls to 0-2, where the search for both a win and a functioning tight end continues.

## 🪙 Survive and Advance: Team Szub 115.22 — Mike's Monstrous Team 114.78

You can criticize Max for carrying three quarterbacks on his roster.

You cannot criticize the results.

Team Szub somehow escapes Week 2 with a **0.44 point win** and moves to 2-0 behind another huge Josh Allen performance.

Allen dropped 40.82 points, while Ja'Marr Chase added 26.5. They needed basically every one of them, because the rest of the lineup wasn't exactly inspiring.

DJ Moore contributed -0.1 points, while Szub's RB1, RB2, and FLEX combined for just 20.1.

Normally that's a recipe for disaster.

This week, it just didn't matter.

Mike's Monstrous Team looked significantly better than the corpse we saw in Week 1. Amon-Ra St. Brown exploded for 35.2, George Kittle added 18, and Omarion Hampton put up 17.5.

But disappointing performances from Justin Herbert and Malik Nabers ultimately left Mike less than half a point short.

Last week Mike got bent over by 66.

This week he lost by 0.44.

I'm honestly not sure which one hurts more.

## 😱 Scary Enough: Joe's Scary Team 126.36 — Johnson 125.52

Apparently Joe took last week's letter personally.

After being declared decidedly not scary in Week 1, Joe's Scary Team responded by putting up 126.36 and stealing one of the closest games of the week.

Dak Prescott led the way with 29.76, James Cook added 20.9, and Sam LaPorta gave Joe some much needed production at tight end.

And he needed all of it.

Johnson got another ridiculous performance from Jaxon Smith-Njigba, who dropped **42.5 points**, while Travis Kelce added another 25.1.

But the injuries are starting to pile up.

Alec Pierce went down, the roster is getting thinner, and Darian now finds himself staring directly at the waiver wire heading into Week 3.

Johnson falls to 0-2, which is especially shocking considering this is the only team in the league to make the playoffs in each of the last three seasons.

That streak isn't dead yet.

But 0-2 isn't exactly where you want to start defending it.

## 🚨 Crime Against Fantasy Football: Team Forde 85.3 — Deez Fadez 73.98

By far the worst game of the week.

85.3 to 73.98.

All but one team in the league would have beaten both of these teams this week. Safe to say this will probably end up being one of the luckiest wins of the year.

I could write a book going position by position and explaining how bad this matchup was, but honestly, it doesn't deserve that much attention.

So I'll focus on the positives.

Team Forde had Brock Purdy sitting on the bench with 28 points.

Deez Fadez somehow had three 20+ point performances sitting on his bench.

And that's about it.

Everything else was absolutely terrible.

Team Forde somehow walks away 2-0 despite putting up 85 points, proving once again that fantasy football isn't always about being good.

Sometimes you just need your opponent to shit the bed harder than you did.

We will now move on and pretend this game never happened.

## 🌊 The Champ Is Underwater: 5280 91.3 — Mele 84.76

The defending champion is officially in trouble.

Mele falls to 0-2 after failing to score 100 points for the second consecutive week.

DeVonta Smith did everything he could, putting up 27.7, but the rest of the lineup apparently decided to watch the game with Mele instead of playing in it.

Two weeks into the title defense, and the defending champ is still waiting for the offense to arrive.

Meanwhile, Carson is going to have a much better Monday at work this week.

After getting beaten by his boss in Week 1, 5280 gets back to .500 behind strong performances from Chris Olave and Christian McCaffrey, who combined for 45.2 points.

The win didn't come without a price.

Jaxson Dart went down with an injury that could have completely derailed Carson's week, but the rest of the lineup did just enough to survive.

For now, it looks like Jared Goff may be getting the call in Week 3 while Carson waits to find out how much time Dart will miss.

After getting cut down by his boss in Week 1, Carson was the one cutting down opponents this week.

## 🐙 Kraken Claims Another Victim: Kraken 137.92 — Team Payne 110.64

What started as a father versus son matchup quickly turned into injured QB versus injured QB.

Both teams watched their QB1 go down in the same week.

Jayden Daniels suffered a nasty elbow injury and looks set to miss some time, although thankfully for Payne it doesn't appear to be season ending.

Then later on Sunday afternoon, Caleb Williams went down and got carted off in a scene that made it look like we had just witnessed a season ending catastrophe.

Turns out he's week to week.

I'll let everyone else decide how they feel about the theatrics.

In the meantime, Kraken just keeps winning.

Garth put up another strong performance with 137.92 points, led by Davante Adams exploding for 39.5 and Dalton Kincaid adding another 22.5.

Kraken moves to 2-0, and Garth takes the first round of this year's battle between father and son while claiming another victim in the process.

The Caleb Williams injury now creates a decision heading into Week 3. Garth can turn to Jordan Love or head to the waiver wire for some insurance while he waits to see when Williams returns.

Payne's afternoon was ugly.

His QB1, tight end, and FLEX all suffered injuries in the middle of their games, completely derailing a matchup that could have looked very different otherwise.

That's fantasy football.

The good news is Payne might have the best insurance policy in the league with Patrick Mahomes sitting there ready to take over at quarterback.

The bad news is Mahomes can't play three positions at once.

Payne heads to the waiver wire.

Kraken heads to 2-0.

And the fantasy football gods head home satisfied after claiming half the league as their Week 2 sacrifice.
