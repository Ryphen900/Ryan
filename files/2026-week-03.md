Three weeks in and the standings have stopped being polite. Here's where we are.

## The game of the week

Kittle Me This and The Punt Life traded the lead four times before a 9-point garbage-time drive settled it. Both benches outscored both starting lineups, which is its own kind of achievement.

## The roast

**Autodraft Andy** put up the lowest score in the league for the second week running and has now been outscored by his own bench by a combined 41 points. At some point that's not variance, that's a lineup card.

**Injured Reserved** is 0-3 while leading the league in points against, which is genuinely unlucky and does not make it any less funny.

## Waiver notes

The top of the wire is thin. If you're streaming a defense, the Week 4 matchups worth targeting are the ones facing backup quarterbacks — check the injury reports Wednesday before you burn FAB on anything.

## Housekeeping

Trade deadline is Week 10. Veto rules haven't changed: it takes six votes, and "I wanted that guy" is not a veto reason.
